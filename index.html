Franchise Opportunity Profile

Jersey Mike's Subs

Made-to-order submarine sandwiches, on-premises and off-premises. Franchisor: A Sub Above, LLC. Every fact on this page is sourced from the Jersey Mike's 2026 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued 2026-04-10, amended 2026-08-26), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$20,000

Non-refundable lump sum per Franchise Agreement. Plus $10,000 development fee per unit under the Area Development Agreement (required regardless of unit count). (FDD Item 5, p.14)

Estimated Initial Investment

$436,176 to $1,162,228

Includes $47,250 to $49,250 paid to the franchisor (development fee + franchise fee + $10,000 grand-opening advertising + POS fees). (FDD cover, p.1; Item 7, p.24)

Royalty

6.5% of Gross Receipts

Payable weekly via wire transfer. (FDD Item 6, p.15)

Advertising Funds

1% corporate + 4% national media

Corporate Advertising and Development Fund: currently 1% (may rise to 6%). National Media Fund: 4%. Combined payments capped at 6% of Gross Receipts. (FDD Item 6, p.15)

Grand Opening Advertising

$10,000

Lump sum at Franchise Agreement signing; not due when purchasing an existing restaurant. Non-refundable. (FDD Item 5, pp.14-15)

Territory

Protected — with broad carve-outs

Company won't establish another physical JERSEY MIKE'S Restaurant in your Designated Territory (if not in default), but reserves non-physical channels (retail, wholesale, online) anywhere, Non-Traditional Venues inside the territory, other businesses under other marks, and acquisitions of competing businesses worldwide. (FDD Item 12, pp.42-43)

Term

10 years + 1 x 10-year renewal

Renewal requires compliance with the Franchise Agreement; $20,000 successor franchise fee. (FDD Item 17, p.48)

Training

3 phases, 8+ weeks

Phases 1 and 3: minimum 8 consecutive weeks on-the-job. Phase 2: 4 days classroom in Manasquan, NJ. 3 trainees; franchisee pays travel. (FDD Item 11, p.36)

HQ

Tinton Falls, New Jersey

1 Commvault Way, Suite 400, Tinton Falls, NJ 07724. (732) 223-4044. Indirect subsidiary of Jersey Mike's Franchise Systems, LLC; Blackstone-managed funds acquired majority ownership January 16, 2025. (FDD Item 1, p.8)

System Size

3,227 outlets

3,201 franchised + 26 company-affiliate at end of fiscal 2025. Franchised count grew from 2,361 at end of 2022 (+35.6%). (FDD Item 20, Table 1, p.58)

Closures

0 terminations disclosed

Zero terminations and zero non-renewals disclosed across all states for fiscal 2023-2025. (FDD Item 20, Table 3)

Franchising Since

1987

Predecessor Jersey Mike's Franchise Systems, LLC offered franchises from February 1987. CEO: Charlie Morrison (since April 2025; ex-Wingstop CEO). (FDD Item 1, p.8; Item 2, p.12)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

Measurement period: January 1, 2025 through December 28, 2025. 3,235 restaurants were open as of December 28, 2025; the figures below cover the 2,606 traditional franchised restaurants included in the data set (621 excluded: 583 open fewer than 361 days, 8 off the designated POS system, 13 non-traditional, 17 company/affiliate/Item-2-person owned).

  • Average unit volume (n=2,606): $1,367,578
  • Median: $1,305,850 · High: $3,228,616 · Low: $512,332
  • 1,148 of 2,606 restaurants (44%) met or exceeded the average
  • Data polled from the designated POS system; the FDD does not state the figures are audited

Unit volume is total sales of food, beverages, merchandise, and services (including off-site and catering), less taxes collected, refunds, discounts, coupons, and donations. Some outlets have sold this amount; individual results may differ; there is no assurance you will do as well. (FDD Item 19, pp.56–57.)

Risk and Disclosure Profile

22 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Lower-moderate. One of the largest U.S. franchise systems (3,227 outlets, franchising since 1987) with zero terminations disclosed in three years and 35.6% franchised growth 2023-2025 — tempered by disclosed litigation (two settlements, one pending class action), a $1.16M investment ceiling, and broad territory carve-outs.

Risk inputs (documented):

  • Litigation: Washington AG no-poach (settled 2019, $150,000); Tsou gift-card class action (settled March 2026, $25,000); Brewer mobile-app service-fee class action PENDING (arbitration granted Aug 2026) — pushes score up
  • Capital intensity: investment ceiling $1,162,228; $47,250-$49,250 paid to franchisor before opening — pushes score up
  • Territory: protected vs other physical restaurants, but Company reserves non-physical channels anywhere, non-traditional venues inside the territory, and acquisitions of competing businesses — pushes score up
  • Item 19 coverage: 2,606 of 3,201 franchised outlets in data set; POS-polled, not stated as audited — moderate
  • Mature system: franchising since 1987; 3,227 outlets; Blackstone majority ownership since January 2025 — pushes score down
  • Unit growth: franchised 2,361 to 3,201 (+35.6%) 2023-2025 — pushes score down
  • Terminations: 0 disclosed 2023-2025 (all states); 0 non-renewals disclosed — pushes score down
  • Bankruptcy: none disclosed (Item 4) — no up-score
